Character exploration

Perch, a quiet notes companion

Three distinct directions were explored, then the strongest one was prototyped in the note window.

1. Lintling

Soft pear-shaped body, long ribbon arms, seed-like eyes, moss and graphite palette, dry-brush sticker texture.

Hangs by folding oversized mitten hands over the note lip. Idle motion is breath, blink, and small leg pendulum.

Selected: the silhouette reads instantly at 48px and the long arms make weight, gripping, dragging, and peeking feel physical.

2. Margin Wisp

Torn-paper wedge body, ink-drop eyes, ivory and blue-black palette, deckled paper grain.

Emerges from the page edge like a note margin came alive. Hands look like folded paper tabs.

Strong connection to writing, but the paper silhouette gets thin and fragile at menu-bar size.

3. Thimble Scout

Compact animal-inspired shape without copying a cat: thimble head, small snout, stub feet, walnut and cream palette.

Hooks paws on the top edge and leans forward to read. Idle motion is cautious head tilts and paw taps.

Appealing and calm, but less original as a product identity than a creature designed specifically for hanging.

Launch thoughts

Keep the writing surface calm. The character should behave like it lives on the note, not beside it.

Prototype beats:

  • Hands appear first, then the body swings down.
  • Typing makes Perch look toward the active line with a delay.
  • Checklist completion earns one small positive bounce.

The main note should always win. Quiet mode trims the idle loops, and Reduce Motion removes the bigger transitions.
